Run with pdfplumber over 30 CVs per tier. Scanned CVs return literally nothing — they exist to force the vision path. The tiers rank reading-order difficulty, not character recovery, which is why "hard" can yield more recoverable text than "easy": a sidebar CV gives you plenty of words, just in an order that makes fields hard to associate. Sub-100% name rates come from uppercase names, and the year gaps from Mar '19 two-digit formats — both deliberate.

Documents carry the depth real CVs do: 3–5 quantified bullets on the most recent role tapering to 1–2 on older ones, thesis titles and coursework for recent graduates, publications for PhDs, certifications with issuer and year, and projects that juniors lean on and seniors mostly drop. Length follows seniority — 841 one-page CVs, 159 two-page, with the long ones concentrated at 8+ years.
